## Generated Wolfy concepts

The files under `generated` were produced for Wolfy with OpenAI image generation, using selected CC0 Notionists characters only as visual references. They propose new clothing, accessories, hairstyles, and reading poses.

These files are concept sheets. They are not part of the original Notionists pack, and they are not ready for runtime use. Their gray checkerboard is baked into the image and is not transparency. Redraw or vectorize an approved concept before shipping it.
